GST Calculator India

Growble's free GST Calculator helps you calculate GST quickly for your business, products and services. You can add GST to an amount or remove GST from a GST-inclusive price.

How to Use the GST Calculator

1. Add GST

Enter the original amount, select the GST rate and the calculator will show the taxable amount, GST amount and final amount.

GST = Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

2. Remove GST

If your amount already includes GST, select "Remove GST". The calculator will determine the taxable value and GST component.

Taxable Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

CGST, SGST and IGST

For an intra-state transaction, the GST amount is divided equally between CGST and SGST. For an inter-state transaction, the GST amount is represented as IGST.

Common GST Rates

This calculator includes 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% GST rates for quick calculations.

Example: 18% GST Calculation

If the taxable amount is ₹10,000 and GST is 18%, the GST amount is ₹1,800 and the total amount is ₹11,800.
