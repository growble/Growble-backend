Privacy Policy

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide CRM services
  • Manage customer leads
  • Send WhatsApp messages through Meta APIs
  • Import Facebook Lead Ads submissions from Pages you connect
  • Improve our services
  • Provide customer support

AI Support Assistant

If you use the in-app support assistant, Growble sends your question and a limited summary of your workspace (plan and expiry, usage totals, seat count, and WhatsApp connection status) to OpenAI to generate an answer, subject to OpenAI's applicable data-handling practices. The assistant does not receive stored lead records, customer conversations, WhatsApp access tokens, passwords, or payment credentials. Obvious email, phone-number, and credential patterns in chat messages are masked before the request is sent to the AI service. Do not include customer information or secrets in your questions. Growble stores a daily question count for rate limiting, but does not store the support chat transcript.

Public Sales Assistant

The public sales assistant on Growble's landing page sends visitor questions and public product information to OpenAI to answer plan and feature questions. It does not collect email addresses or other contact details and does not store chat transcripts. Obvious email, phone-number, and credential patterns are masked before AI processing. To enforce a limit of five questions per visitor each UTC day, Growble stores a one-way HMAC of the visitor IP address and a daily count until the daily limit resets; the raw IP address is not stored for this feature. Do not include customer information, passwords, or payment details in questions.

Third-Party Services

Growble integrates with Meta WhatsApp Cloud API and Facebook Lead Ads, Razorpay, OpenAI, Google Analytics and other services required to operate the platform. If you connect a Facebook Page, Growble encrypts and stores its Page access token to receive and retrieve lead form submissions, and stores the lead details you choose to collect in your CRM. You can disconnect a Page in Growble or revoke Growble’s access from your Meta Business Integrations settings.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
