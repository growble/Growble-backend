Connect Facebook Lead Ads

Authorize your Facebook account, choose the Pages that run lead forms, and Growble will add new form submissions to your leads dashboard.

Available on Pro and Agent. Facebook Lead Ads forms must collect a phone number. Meta may require app review and the leads_retrieval permission before this works for other people’s Pages. Configure the Meta Page webhook for the leadgen field and set META_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY to a persistent 32-byte hexadecimal key.
